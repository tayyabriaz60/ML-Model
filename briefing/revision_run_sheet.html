    When to escalate rather than proceed

    Report to the author promptly, not at the end. Each of these is a case where continuing produces work that has to be redone.

    • The audit does not reproduce the submitted numbers. The diagnosis is wrong, and §4.1, §6.1 and the letter's opening all need rewriting. Five days no longer holds.

    • A central claim no longer holds once the numbers land. Better to extend than to ship a claim nobody checked.
